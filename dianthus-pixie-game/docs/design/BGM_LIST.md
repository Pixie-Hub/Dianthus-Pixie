# Track List

### 1. `exploration_day.mp3` — Exploration Phase (Day / Morning)
- **Mood:** Peaceful, curious, warm, hopeful
- **Instrumentation:** Acoustic guitar or ukulele lead, light woodwind (flute or recorder), soft hand percussion, gentle nature ambience underneath
- **Tempo:** ~90 BPM, relaxed
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (seamless loop)
- **Notes:** This plays while the player forages and explores. It should feel like a cheerful countryside morning — Indonesian botanical garden vibes. No percussion intensity. Bright major key.

---

### 2. `preparation_phase.mp3` — Preparation Phase (Late Afternoon / Dusk)
- **Mood:** Slightly tense, focused, anticipatory — not yet scary, but the player knows night is coming
- **Instrumentation:** Underscore with light strings or picked guitar, a soft synth pad underneath beginning to shift toward minor, subtle ticking or rhythmic pulse
- **Tempo:** ~100 BPM, building
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (seamless loop)
- **Notes:** Transitions emotionally from the warmth of daytime into an uneasy dusk. Should feel like "something is coming." Can use a brief harmonic shift from major to relative minor near the end before looping.

---

### 3. `night_combat_base.mp3` — Night / Defense Phase (Base Layer)
- **Mood:** Tense, dark, rhythmically driven — survival urgency
- **Instrumentation:** Synth bass / dark synth pulse, tribal or electronic percussion, a faint melodic plant-themed counter-melody (e.g., a haunting flute or vibraphone line weaving under the percussion)
- **Tempo:** ~130 BPM, driving
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (seamless loop)
- **Notes:** This is the base layer for night combat. It should work as a standalone loop that sounds intense but not overwhelming — it will have a second intensity layer added on top when enemy count is high.

---

### 4. `night_combat_intense.mp3` — Night / Defense Phase (High Intensity Layer)
- **Mood:** Overwhelming, desperate, peak urgency
- **Instrumentation:** Add aggressive synth stabs, faster hi-hats or glitch percussion on top of the base layer, distorted bass swell, dissonant choir or string stabs
- **Tempo:** ~130 BPM (same as base layer — must sync perfectly for dynamic layering)
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (must be perfectly sync-able with `night_combat_base.mp3`)
- **Notes:** This layer plays ON TOP of `night_combat_base.mp3` when more than 50% of enemies in a wave are still alive. Design it so it can fade in/out cleanly. Do NOT re-include the base percussion — only the additive intensity elements.

---

### 5. `surge_night.mp3` — Surge Night / Boss Wave (Every 7th Night)
- **Mood:** Epic, overwhelming dread, peak drama
- **Instrumentation:** Full orchestral + synth hybrid — brass stabs, pounding timpani, dark choir, heavy synth bass, a corrupted version of the day theme as a counter-melody
- **Tempo:** ~140 BPM
- **Duration:** 60 seconds (two 30-second segments: first half builds, second half is peak intensity, both loop-able)
- **Notes:** The 7th night sends 3× the normal enemy count. Music should reflect existential threat. The corrupted day-theme counter-melody is a key storytelling device.

---

### 6. `devourer_boss.mp3` — The Devourer Final Boss
- **Mood:** Ancient evil, cosmic dread, yet laced with a bittersweet Dianthus motif
- **Instrumentation:** Deep ambient drone bass, evolving synth layers, haunting vocal or synthetic choir, a fragile piano or music box counter-melody representing the Dianthus (hope within darkness), explosive percussion when boss phases transition
- **Tempo:** Fluid — approximately 110 BPM with rubato sections
- **Duration:** 90 seconds (three 30-second segments for 3 boss phases)
- **Segment breakdown:**
  - 0–30s (Phase 1): Eerie, measured, probing — the Devourer approaches
  - 30–60s (Phase 2): Chaos increases — synth stabs, faster percussion, the Dianthus motif becomes strained
  - 60–90s (Phase 3): Climactic — full ensemble, choir, the Dianthus motif emerges triumphant or tragically depending on the fight outcome
- **Notes:** This is the emotional centerpiece of the entire game. Take care with the Dianthus motif — it should be recognizable from the day theme, transformed.

---

### 7. `main_menu.mp3` — Main Menu / Title Screen
- **Mood:** Mysterious, inviting, teasing the dual nature of the game (nature + darkness)
- **Instrumentation:** A gentle piano or acoustic guitar melody over a slow, evolving ambient synth pad; faint echoes of both the day theme and a dark harmonic undertone
- **Tempo:** ~75 BPM, slow and atmospheric
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (seamless loop)
- **Notes:** First impression of the game. Should feel like standing at the edge of a magical garden at twilight — beautiful but with a hint of unease.

---

### 8. `ending_true.mp3` — True Ending Theme
- **Mood:** Triumphant, emotional, resolution — bittersweet but ultimately hopeful
- **Instrumentation:** Full arrangement of the main Dianthus motif — strings, piano, acoustic guitar, light choir, soft percussion
- **Tempo:** ~85 BPM
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (no loop needed — plays once on ending screen)
- **Notes:** The player has survived 30+ nights and defeated The Devourer. This should feel earned. Draw from the day exploration theme as a full-circle moment.

---

### 9. `ending_survival.mp3` — Survival Ending Theme
- **Mood:** Reflective, melancholic, open-ended — "survived but the darkness lingers"
- **Instrumentation:** Solo acoustic guitar or piano, sparse, unresolved harmonic tension
- **Tempo:** ~70 BPM
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (no loop needed)

---

### 10. `ending_discovery.mp3` — Discovery Ending Theme
- **Mood:** Wonder, curiosity fulfilled, gentle magic
- **Instrumentation:** Playful woodwind + light piano, evolving into a warm major resolution
- **Tempo:** ~90 BPM
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (no loop needed)
