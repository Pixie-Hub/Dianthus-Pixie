# Dianthus Pixie Teaser Video Script

**Context:** Final project teaser for GIGA Laboratory Open Recruitment at ITS
**Deadline:** May 15, 2026, 11:59 PM
**Target length:** 45-60 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 gameplay teaser with quick title cards, in-game footage, and music/SFX
**Primary goal:** Show the core fantasy and loop clearly: explore by day, craft and grow stronger, defend the Dianthus Core at night.

---

## What To Include

The teaser should focus on player-facing moments that are already recognizable in the game:

- **Game identity:** title screen or title card for "Dianthus Pixie".
- **Core premise:** protect the Dianthus Core from night threats.
- **Daytime exploration:** Meadow Edge routes, resource pickup, rare nodes, map movement.
- **Preparation loop:** crafting, cross-breeding, inventory, plant placement, fortification building.
- **Combat:** Thorn Sword, Spore Bomb, Vine Whip, Petal Shield, enemy hits, wave pressure.
- **Plant fantasy:** placed plants supporting the player, energy gain, garden growth, Core glow.
- **Night defense:** wave counter, enemy lanes, Core HP, urgent defense footage.
- **Progression hook:** new zones, stronger enemies, Devourer/boss tease, endings or endless mode hint.
- **Final callout:** GIGA Laboratory Open Recruitment at ITS final project.

Avoid spending too much time on menus or static UI. Use UI only when it helps explain the loop: inventory, crafting, quest objective, minimap, wave counter, Core HP.

---

## Teaser Structure

### 0:00-0:04 - Cold Open

**Visual:** Black screen fades into the glowing Dianthus Core at night.
**Audio:** Low ambient music, soft Core pulse, distant enemy sound.

**On-screen text:**

```text
When night falls...
the garden must survive.
```

**Editing note:** Start quiet. Let the Core glow be the first visual signal.

---

### 0:04-0:10 - Game Title Reveal

**Visual:** Quick montage: player in Meadow Edge, Dianthus Core, garden plants, enemies approaching. Cut to title.

**On-screen text:**

```text
DIANTHUS PIXIE
```

**Voice-over optional:**

```text
In Dianthus Pixie, every day is a chance to prepare...
and every night is a test of survival.
```

---

### 0:10-0:20 - Explore And Gather

**Visual checklist:**

- Player leaving the garden.
- Meadow Edge route traversal.
- Petal Shard and Verdant Sap pickups.
- Rare resource node or Harvest QTE.
- Minimap or objective panel briefly visible.

**On-screen text:**

```text
Explore the wilds.
Gather what the garden needs.
```

**Shot direction:** Use fast 1-2 second cuts. Show movement and pickups clearly, not just scenery.

---

### 0:20-0:31 - Craft, Breed, And Prepare

**Visual checklist:**

- Inventory or resource grid.
- Crafting screen with weapon/result preview.
- Cross-breeding screen or plant discovery moment.
- Plant placement near the Core.
- Fortification build interaction if available.

**On-screen text:**

```text
Craft living weapons.
Breed powerful plants.
Build your defenses.
```

**Voice-over optional:**

```text
Resources become weapons. Plants become strategy. The garden becomes your last line of defense.
```

**Editing note:** This section should feel like calm preparation before the night cut.

---

### 0:31-0:45 - Survive The Night

**Visual checklist:**

- Phase shift to night.
- Enemies entering from lanes.
- Player attacking with at least two weapons.
- Plants or traps damaging/slowing enemies.
- Core HP bar visible during pressure.
- Wave counter or tactical forecast/minimap arrows if readable.

**On-screen text:**

```text
Then defend the Dianthus Core.
```

**Music/SFX direction:** Increase intensity here. Layer combat hits, enemy damage, Core warning, and defense music.

**Editing note:** Use the strongest gameplay footage here. Prioritize clarity: player, enemy, Core, hit feedback.

---

### 0:45-0:54 - Escalation Hook

**Visual checklist:**

- Dusk Forest or Ruins of Veld shot.
- Stronger enemy types.
- Devourer boss tease or ominous cutscene shot.
- Story/quest text only if it is short and readable.

**On-screen text:**

```text
Survive longer.
Uncover the threat.
Face what waits beyond the garden.
```

**Editing note:** Do not fully explain the story. Tease scale and danger.

---

### 0:54-1:00 - End Card

**Visual:** Title card over Core/garden footage or clean dark background with subtle Core glow.

**On-screen text:**

```text
DIANTHUS PIXIE
GIGA Laboratory Open Recruitment Final Project
Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember
```

**Optional small footer:**

```text
Built in Godot 4.6
```

**Audio:** End on a short musical hit or Core pulse.

## Editing Notes

- Keep the teaser short. A 60-second polished teaser is better than a long feature list.
- Capture footage at consistent resolution and frame rate.
- Hide debug overlays unless the clip specifically needs them.
- Use readable title cards with high contrast.
- Do not show unfinished placeholder art for too long. If unavoidable, cut quickly and focus on gameplay action.
- Start with atmosphere, build through preparation, peak at night defense, then end with the project identity.
- Use the strongest sound moments: pickup feedback, crafting confirmation, weapon hit, enemy hit, Core pulse, night music.
